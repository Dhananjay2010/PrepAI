# 14 — Post-Launch Iteration Ideas

**Prerequisite:** you've actually launched and files 01–13 are live in production. Don't read this as a to-do list before then — it'll just distract you from shipping the MVP.
**Produces:** nothing yet — this is a backlog for after you have real user feedback.

---

Once PrepAI is live and you've gathered some initial usage and feedback, these are natural next features, roughly in order of expected impact:

- **Resume + JD gap analysis mode** — upload a resume alongside the JD, identify gaps, generate "bridge answers" for how to position existing experience. Significantly raises willingness to pay since it's more personalized than JD-only prep.
- **Company-specific question banks** — e.g. "Amazon interview style," "startup system design style." Requires curated prompt variants per company archetype.
- **Chrome extension** — right-click a job posting on LinkedIn/Naukri → generate questions instantly without copy-pasting. Removes friction, likely increases conversion.
- **Referral system** — free month for inviting 3 friends. Cheap growth lever once you have any paying users at all.
- **B2B mode** — bulk question generation for coding bootcamps or colleges. One institutional client at ₹2000+/month can beat dozens of individual free-to-paid conversions, and your teaching background is a real credibility advantage in these sales conversations.

**Before building any of these:** check your actual usage data (PostHog, from file 12) and direct user feedback first. Build what users are actually asking for or dropping off from, not what seems interesting to build.
